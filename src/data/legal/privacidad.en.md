At Carinova we respect your privacy. This policy explains which personal data we handle and what for.

## Data we receive

- What you enter when you open your mailbox on this site: country, first and last name, e-mail address, phone and address, and the language you used the site in.
- What you enter in the form for companies: the name of your company, your name, your e-mail, your phone, your country and your city, and what you tell us about your shipments.
- What you give us when you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, e-mail or at a branch, and the information about your packages.
- The details of your purchases that we receive from stores and carriers (for example, tracking number and description of the contents), which are needed for shipping and customs.

## What we use it for

- To create your account, assign your Carinova ID and send you your address in Miami.
- To let you sign in to your mailbox and show you your details.
- To manage your shipments and let you know the status of your packages.
- To answer your questions and the requests of companies.
- To meet customs and legal obligations in Nicaragua and in the United States.

## Who we share it with

- With those who take part in the shipment (carriers, agents and customs authorities) and when the law requires it.
- With the providers that make this site work, which only handle it on our behalf: the hosting of the site (Cloudflare), the sending of e-mail (Resend) and the database where we keep the accounts (Neon). Their servers may be outside Nicaragua.

We do not sell or rent your data.

## Signing up on this site

When you submit the sign-up form we send you an e-mail with a link to confirm that the address is yours. Your account is created only when you open that link and confirm: if you do not, no account is created. The link expires after 48 hours.

## Signing in to your mailbox

You do not use a password to sign in: you enter your e-mail and we send you a 6-digit code, which expires after 10 minutes and works only once. We do not keep the code itself, only a fingerprint from which it cannot be rebuilt.

When you sign in, your browser stores a session cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days or until you sign out. That cookie only serves to recognise you on this site: other sites and the page's own scripts cannot read it, and on our side we keep only a fingerprint of it. If you use a shared device, sign out when you finish.

## The form for companies

What you enter in the form for companies is sent by e-mail to our team so that we can contact you. We do not use it for anything else.

## This website

This site does not use tracking cookies or advertising tools. The only cookies it uses are those of your session when you sign in to your mailbox, which are necessary for it to work. Our hosting provider may record technical data about visits (such as the IP address) for security. If we add analytics tools in the future, we will say so here.

When you use a WhatsApp link or open a store from this site, you leave it: what you do there is governed by the policies of WhatsApp or of that store.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data on WhatsApp or at any of our branches.

## Changes

If we update this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its date.
